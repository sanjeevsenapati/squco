01 — The Nostalgic Memory

Remember When
Everyone Carried One?

Carrying a handkerchief was once a normal everyday habit. It was practical, personal, hygienic, and reflected care for oneself and others.

"Today we carry phones, wallets, cards, earbuds and power banks. But somehow, we stopped carrying something much simpler."
  • In school bags & pockets
  • To the office & meetings
  • During travel & commutes
  • At weddings & family dates
  • For children & loved ones
  • For life's quiet moments

Maybe it's time to bring it back.

02 — Why Did We Stop?

Why Did We Stop Carrying Hankies?

Tissues became more convenient. Pocket spaces changed. Disposable culture became normal. People simply stopped thinking about carrying a hanky.

"Old Habit. New Attitude."

SQUCO isn't trying to bring back the past exactly as it was. We are taking something genuinely useful from the past and redesigning it for today — modern design, compact packaging, beautiful presentation, and easy WhatsApp ordering.
