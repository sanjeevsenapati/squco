# SQUCO — The Handkerchief Revival

Build a **premium, emotionally engaging, content-first static website** for **SQUCO**, an exclusive modern handkerchief brand.

The website is not simply an e-commerce product catalogue.

The central idea behind SQUCO is:

> **People forgot the handkerchief. SQUCO wants to bring it back.**

For generations, carrying a handkerchief was a normal everyday habit. It was practical, personal, hygienic and often reflected personality and culture. Today, many people have replaced this simple habit with disposable tissues and other alternatives.

**SQUCO wants to restore the habit of carrying and using a handkerchief in modern society.**

The website should communicate this mission strongly.

---

# 1. Core Brand Philosophy

## Brand

**SQUCO**

## Brand Category

Premium / modern handkerchief brand.

## Core Mission

**Bring the handkerchief back into everyday life.**

The website should make visitors think:

> “I actually used to carry a handkerchief. Why did I stop?”

And then:

> “I should start carrying one again.”

This emotional transition is more important than simply showing products.

Build the entire website around a central message:

# 3. The Unique SQUCO Concept

SQUCO should have a distinctive physical identity.

The handkerchief should come in an extremely compact:

## **2 × 2 × 2 inches packet**

This tiny packet becomes an important part of the brand story.

The website should visually communicate the contrast:

**Tiny packet → Useful everyday habit**

The concept:

> “A handkerchief small enough to forget in your pocket, but useful enough to remember every day.”

Show the 2×2×2 inches packet prominently.

The packet should feel like a modern lifestyle object rather than traditional packaging.

---
